Our Mission

A woman-led record label bringing passionate stories, songs, and films to life through a multicultural lens.

We champion Latin, Caribbean, and Asian voices, East and South, with characters who carry their heritage with them: not as a backdrop, but as the heart of the story.

The Roster

Meet the roster

The voices of the court.

AI Creative Director · Artist, Author & Filmmaker

Reina Acevedo

Music Books Film

The hopeless-romantic side of our founder, DC, brought to life with AI.

Reina is the label's AI creative director and the first artist on its roster. Her voice starts with DC's own vocals, refined with Suno, and she sings in English, Spanish, and Hindi. She is the author of the romance novel Signed in Blood and directs and stars in our films, from the fantasy series Mark of the Flame to the music video Distance Within Time (Reimagined).

Her story draws on DC's own Puerto Rican and Indo-Guyanese roots, and her music moves between flamenco, bolero, Bollywood rhythms, and dark pop.

“Reina sings the languages I grew up hearing but never learned to speak.”DC, Founder

AI Recording Artist

Shara Rayne

Music Film

Shara Rayne is an AI vocalist known for a breathy, angelic soprano and lush stacked harmonies that never belt, never scream, and never change.

She carries that voice across nu-metal, gothic rock, dark-pop and Mandarin-language Mandopop, with roots in synthwave, dream-pop and trance. Her songs are about grief, complicated love and finding your own name, written and produced by RG4M3. She also stars in the film series Mark of the Flame.

The Founders

Meet the founders

The vision behind the court.

DC
Founder

DC

DC has spent a decade in tech, working across media, e-commerce, and affiliate and influencer marketing. Court of Love Records brings that experience to a love of storytelling, using AI tools to empower creatives and show just how far storytelling can go.

DC writes, films, and edits every release. Reina Acevedo is DC's creative alter ego: the hopeless romantic who sings, writes, and makes films, given a voice and a face through AI.

RC
Co-Founder

RC

RC spent a decade in the media industry before taking that experience into music production, where he works under the moniker RG4M3. As co-founder, he leads the business side of the label, guiding key decisions and heading up outreach to the artists, creators, and partners we collaborate with.
