Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

The short version: this website doesn't ask for, collect, or sell your personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms, no tracking cookies, and no advertising. If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

This Privacy Policy explains how Court of Love Records ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you visit courtofloverecords.com (the "Site").

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the Site. The Site has no forms, accounts, comment sections, or checkout, and it does not use analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies.

The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us by email, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

How we use email you send us

If you contact us at info@courtofloverecords.com, we use your information only to read and respond to your inquiry, such as a collaboration, commission, licensing, or press request. We do not add you to a mailing list, sell or rent your information, or share it with anyone outside Court of Love Records except where required by law.

Third-party services

Like nearly every website, the Site relies on a few outside services to load. These providers may receive basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, as part of delivering their service:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the Site. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the Site's typefaces. Your browser requests the fonts from Google's servers. See the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not control these services and do not receive the information they process.

Links to other sites

The Site links to platforms such as Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Spotify, and Higgsfield. When you follow those links, you leave our Site, and that platform's own privacy policy applies.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete any emails or information you have sent us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under privacy laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act. We honor these requests regardless of where you live. Email info@courtofloverecords.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Data security and retention

We keep email correspondence only as long as needed to respond and maintain a record of our conversations, and we take reasonable steps to protect it.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that collect information, such as a newsletter sign-up or analytics, we will update this policy before doing so and change the effective date above.
